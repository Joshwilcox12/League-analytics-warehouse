#League Analytics Warehouse
## Business Problem

League players have access to their match history but struggle to get the most out of it to help them improve at the game.

## Goal

Provide insights that help players imporve their game play and rank by providing insightful data using match history.

## User Stories

As a player 

- I want to see my match history

- I want to know my best champ

- I want to know if my cs is improving

- I want to know which champions I should stop playing

- I want to know if I perform better at a certain time or day

- I want to compare performace at start of season and at end of season

- I want to learn what champs I am bad vs and should ban it


## Metrics

cs/min

kda

win rate

lp gains/changes

games played

vision score

time played

damage/min

## Questions the Warehouse Must Answer

- What champion has the highest win rate?
- What champion has the highest KDA?
- Am I improving over the last 20 games?
- Which role should I queue?
- Do I play worse after midnight?
- Which champion matchup is hardest?
