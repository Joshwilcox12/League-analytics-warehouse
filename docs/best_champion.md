# Best Champions



## Business Question
I want to know which champions I perform best on in League of Legends.


## Why it Matters

Knowing which champions a player performs best on helps them identify which champions give them the highest chance of climbing the ranked ladder. It also allows players to measure whether they are improving on the champions they invest time in.



## Metrics

- cs/min
- kda
- win rate
- games played
